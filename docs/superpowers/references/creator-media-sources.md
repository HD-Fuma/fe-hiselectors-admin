# Korean creator media sources

## Provenance and usage

- Generated on: 2026-08-06
- Source: OpenAI built-in image generation tool (`image_gen`), followed by local deterministic normalization with macOS `sips`
- Subjects: four fictional adult Korean women, created for the Selectors admin UI. They do not depict or intentionally resemble real creators, celebrities, or other known people.
- Endorsement: the images do not imply a real-person, creator, product, café, platform, or brand endorsement.
- Final format: 16 sRGB JPEG files, each 640 × 640 pixels.
- Identity method: each creator's profile portrait was generated first. Its local PNG was then supplied as the identity reference for that creator's three post images.

## Shared art direction and constraints

All images used the `photorealistic-natural` use case and square creator/profile or social-post framing. Prompts required realistic skin pores, hair, fabric, food, and environmental texture; clearly adult subjects; original fictional identities; natural anatomy; and no celebrity resemblance. Every prompt prohibited minors, logos, watermarks, readable text, plastic-looking skin, malformed faces or hands, extra fingers, and duplicate people. Posts explicitly required preservation of the referenced profile identity. Phone UI, product labels, and signage were required to be blank or unreadable.
